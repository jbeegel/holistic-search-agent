Every surface has an ownerNone of this is unowned. The issue is that no one currently owns them for citation, because citation was not a goal when the responsibilities were assigned.
CS = Customer SuccessThe team that manages existing customers. They own the relationship, so they own the review ask. Search should never email a customer directly.
What "not tracked" meansCommunity threads are heavily cited by engines but nobody is watching them, so factual errors about the product sit uncorrected for months.
Paid is the highest-fidelity input you already own. Converting paid query data is the best available definition of the prompt set worth tracking for citation. That makes paid and organic a shared roadmap rather than a reporting line, and it is the fastest integration to stand up because both already report to the same leader.
What to do about it

Three of six surfaces have no owner. The proposal is a standing citation council: search, brand, CS, and paid, biweekly against one shared scorecard, with citation-source mix as the agenda rather than each team's channel metrics.
